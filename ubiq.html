002 / What is UBIQ

The AI that
follows you.

UBIQ is Hypermind's local intelligence platform — a sovereign AI engine that runs entirely on your hardware. No subscriptions to third-party clouds, no data sent anywhere. Just raw, private intelligence aligned to your rules.

Deploy on any device. Customise every behaviour. Own every output. UBIQ puts Local Intelligence where it belongs — with you.

003 / Capabilities

Built different,
from the ground up.

Every design decision in UBIQ starts from one principle: your AI should serve you, not surveil you.

01

100% Private

Your data never leaves your device. No telemetry, no logging, no silent uploads. What happens in UBIQ stays in UBIQ.

02

Any Device

Laptop, workstation, edge server — UBIQ adapts to your hardware and runs optimally on everything from consumer GPUs to enterprise clusters.

03

Fully Customisable

Fine-tune models, set your own system prompts, define custom tools. UBIQ is a foundation you build on — not a black box you submit to.

04

No Latency

Skip the round-trip to a distant data centre. Local inference means sub-second responses, even when you're offline or air-gapped.

05

Open Stack

Built on open standards. Bring your own models, integrate with your existing tools, and stay independent of any single provider forever.

06

Team-Ready

Deploy UBIQ across your organisation with role-based access, shared knowledge bases, and centralised configuration — all on-prem.
